AIR QUALITY

AQI Categories

Understand what different AQI levels mean for health.

01

Good

0 – 50

Air quality is considered good and poses little or no risk.

02

Satisfactory

51 – 100

Air quality is acceptable, although sensitive people may experience minor effects.

03

Moderate

101 – 200

Sensitive people may experience health discomfort.

04

Poor

201 – 300

Prolonged exposure may affect sensitive and vulnerable groups.

05

Very Poor

301 – 400

Increased risk of respiratory and other health effects.

06

Severe

401+

Serious health effects are possible.
